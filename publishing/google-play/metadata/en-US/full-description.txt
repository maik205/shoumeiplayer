Shoumei Player is a native Android TV client for your personal Jellyfin media server.

Designed for the living room, it provides remote-first navigation, cinematic browsing, library search, detailed movie and series pages, and native playback powered by libmpv.

Browse your libraries, continue unfinished videos, choose audio and subtitle tracks, and report playback progress directly to your Jellyfin server.

Shoumei Player does not provide, host, sell, or distribute media. A separately configured Jellyfin server and access to your own media library are required.
